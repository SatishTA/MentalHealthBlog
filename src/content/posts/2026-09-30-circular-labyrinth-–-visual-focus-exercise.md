  Sit comfortably in a quiet place in front of the Circular Labyrinth.

  Start at the entrance and slowly follow the path with your eyes until you reach the exit.

  If you find it difficult to follow the path, draw small arrows along the way to guide yourself toward the exit.

  Practice a few times using the arrows. Once you feel comfortable, try following the path again without the arrows.
